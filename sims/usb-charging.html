Plug the same phone into a wall charger and into a laptop's USB port and one of them takes all afternoon. It is tempting to blame the cable, or to assume the slow port is pushing fewer volts. It isn't. Both ports sit at exactly 5.0 volts — what differs is how much current each one will allow.

Same wall socket. Same 5.0 V. Watch the current gauges, not the voltage.

Simplified: real charging tapers off near full (CC-CV), and many modern fast chargers raise voltage as well as current (USB-PD, Quick Charge) — 5 V is held fixed on both sides here specifically to isolate the current-limiting effect. Battery energy assumed at 11 Wh, a rounded stand-in for a typical ~3000 mAh phone battery.
